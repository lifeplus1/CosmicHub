**Traditional Foundation**: TCM represents over 3,000 years of accumulated medical wisdom, rooted in
Taoist philosophy and classical Chinese cosmology. The system views health through the lens of
harmony between opposing forces (Yin-Yang) and the dynamic flow of vital energy (Qi) through
specific pathways (meridians).

**Core Philosophical Principles**:

- **Wu Xing (Five Elements)**: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water - representing dynamic cycles of
  creation and destruction
- **Yin-Yang Theory**: Complementary opposites maintaining universal balance
- **Qi Theory**: Vital energy flowing through 12 primary meridians
- **Zang-Fu System**: Organ networks encompassing physical, emotional, and spiritual functions

## 🕉️ SECTION 2: Ayurveda Cultural Authentication

### Historical and Cultural Context

**Sacred Foundation**: Ayurveda ("knowledge of life") represents humanity's oldest medical system,
originating from the Vedic tradition over 5,000 years ago. Rooted in the concept of cosmic
consciousness (Brahman) manifesting through individual constitution (Prakriti), Ayurveda views
health as harmony between microcosm and macrocosm.

**Core Philosophical Principles**:

- **Pancha Mahabhuta (Five Elements)**: Space, Air, Fire, Water, Earth
- **Tridosha Theory**: Three biological energies (Vata, Pitta, Kapha) governing all physiological
  and psychological functions
- **Prakriti-Vikriti**: Birth constitution vs. current imbalanced state
- **Ojas-Tejas-Prana**: The three vital essences maintaining life force
